# Master Entry-Level Healthcare Evidence Coverage System™

## Purpose

This section is the controlled career-evidence research layer for Kori Pickle's Healthcare Operations Proof System.

Its purpose is to answer two separate questions with evidence instead of assumptions:

1. **Market evidence:** What employer evidence establishes that a competency matters for the entry-level role family being studied?
2. **Personal evidence:** What verified artifact evidence establishes that Kori can legitimately claim preparation in that competency?

If either side is missing, the competency row is not complete.

> **Current phase:** Employer-side baseline research only. Do not build or score the Master Evidence Coverage Matrix until the RF-01 market sample has been collected, classified, normalized, and frequency-tested.

## Current Role Family

### RF-01 — Patient Access

Patient Access is the first role family because it overlaps with existing student-developed healthcare operations work involving registration workflow, demographic accuracy, eligibility, insurance information, documentation, patient communication, work queues, follow-up, escalation, workflow ownership, data quality, and revenue-cycle readiness.

The purpose of RF-01 is to test the evidence methodology before expanding it to RF-02 through RF-09.

---

## Locked Research Sequence

1. Lock RF-01 Patient Access.
2. Collect 10–15 current Patient Access job postings.
3. Preserve original employer requirement language.
4. Classify each requirement.
5. Normalize similar requirements into competencies.
6. Calculate market frequency.
7. Audit existing evidence against each competency.
8. Assign evidence classification A / B / C / D / GAP.
9. Score 0–4 student evidence strength.
10. Test Content Ready and Public Access Verified separately.
11. Classify true gaps.
12. Assign LEARN / PRACTICE / PACKAGE / WORKPLACE-DEPENDENT.
13. Build the Patient Access coverage dashboard.
14. Validate the methodology.
15. Repeat for RF-02 through RF-09.

### Current stop point

Only Steps 1–6 are permitted in the current phase. Steps 7–15 begin only after the employer requirement baseline is complete.

## Job-Posting Inclusion Rules

The primary market sample should prioritize postings that are:

- 100% remote
- Tennessee eligible
- free of mandatory onsite training
- from a legitimate employer and source
- active at the time of review
- genuinely entry-level or reasonably accessible

A posting that fails Kori's personal job-search filters may still be retained as **supplemental market intelligence** if it contains useful requirement language, but it must be clearly marked as not personally eligible and must not be counted as a qualified posting without explanation.

---

## Employer-Language Preservation Rule

Do not normalize employer wording during collection.

The order is:

**Original employer language → Requirement classification → Normalized competency → Frequency calculation → Personal evidence crosswalk**

The original employer language remains in the research layer as the audit trail.

## Future Evidence Classifications

These classifications will be used only after the employer baseline is complete:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Verified resume / source-of-truth evidence |
| B | Student-developed portfolio evidence |
| C | Coursework / learning evidence |
| D | Verified transferable evidence |
| GAP | No defensible evidence |

These codes describe **what the evidence is**. They do not describe evidence strength.

---

## Future Student Evidence Strength Scale

This scale will not be applied until the market baseline is complete.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | GAP — no defensible evidence |
| 1 | Awareness — coursework / learning only |
| 2 | Supporting — related but indirect evidence |
| 3 | Strong Student Evidence — direct role-related simulated artifact with explainable contribution |
| 4 | Recruiter-Ready Student Evidence — direct artifact + verified contribution + boundary statement + recruiter wording + interview explanation + accessible proof |

A score of 4 means the strongest defensible **student-level** evidence. It does not mean professional experience.

---

## Integrity Rules

- Never convert a professional-experience requirement into a portfolio claim.
- Never represent simulated work as employment experience.
- Never claim Epic, EHR, payer-portal, claims-processing, coding, management, clinical, compliance, or payer authority unless independently verified.
- Keep student-developed, simulated, educational, and no-PHI boundaries explicit.
- Separate **Content Ready** from **Public Recruiter Access Verified**.
- Never build a new project simply because a recruiter-access or packaging problem exists.
- Workplace-dependent gaps may remain gaps.
